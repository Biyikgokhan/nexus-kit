# Governance

Nexus Interlink is a maintainer-led open-source project initiated by Gökhan Bıyık, not an incorporated foundation. The long-term ambition is useful open tools, independent creators and transparent stewardship.

Decisions happen through issues and PRs. Maintainers review accessibility, interoperability, privacy and asset rights, and record tradeoffs. Contributors retain copyright under the project licenses. Disagreements can be raised in an issue with a concrete alternative.

No central account is needed to create or host a compatible place. Registry inclusion is optional and reviewed. Direct links work without registry approval. Inclusion is not proof of business ownership or endorsement.

Contributors may help with code, translations, assets and registry reviews. New maintainers require sustained contributions, explicit consent and scoped access. Financial sponsorship, a council and legal incorporation remain future decisions.

Be respectful. No harassment, impersonation, doxxing, unlicensed submissions or spam. Maintainers may remove harmful contributions and should explain decisions and allow appeals through issues or private contact when needed.
